Description

The Professional Standard for Secure Tablet Mounting

Elevate your digital interface with the SlateSafe™ Enclosure, a precision-engineered mounting solution designed exclusively for the Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+. Whether you are deploying a high-traffic Point-of-Sale (POS) system, a corporate visitor check-in kiosk, or a dedicated Home Assistant dashboard, SlateSafe provides a secure, sleek, and fully functional housing that looks like part of the architecture, not an afterthought.

Engineered for Performance & Security

  • Tamper-Resistant Security: Our fully enclosed design protects your hardware in public and commercial spaces, ensuring your device stays exactly where it belongs.
  • Full Hardware Access: Unlike “universal” mounts that block essential features, SlateSafe provides dedicated access to power and volume buttons, as well as precision cutouts for front and rear cameras.
  • Crystal Clear Communication: Designed specifically for video conferencing, the enclosure features unobstructed front-facing microphone access, ensuring your audio remains crisp for every call or voice command.
  • Ultra-Slim, Modern Bezel: We believe the best mount is the one you don't notice. Our slim-profile bezel maintains the modern aesthetic of the Tab A9+ while providing rugged protection.
  • USB-C Cable Included: Getting your tablet set-up and running should be an out-of-box experience. We include a flat USB-C cable that perfectly routes within the case in this kit.

The Cleanest Install on the Market

Every SlateSafe enclosure is built around our signature Low-Profile USB-C Cable (included). This custom right-angle cable allows the tablet to sit nearly flush against the wall, eliminating the bulk and clutter of standard wiring.

  • Integrated Cable Management: A rear exit port ensures all wiring is hidden from view for a professional, “wireless” look.
  • PoE Ready: The included USB-C cable supports both power and USB 2.0 data speeds, making it fully compatible with PoE (Power over Ethernet) adapters for a simplified, single-cable deployment.

Versatile Mounting Options

Designed to adapt to your environment, the SlateSafe enclosure is VESA 75mm compatible. It can be mounted to any standard desktop stand or arm, or fixed directly to a wall.
