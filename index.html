Engineered for the Slimmest Profile

The secret to a professional installation isn't just the case—it's the cable.

Most tablet enclosures are bulky because they are forced to hide thick, rounded consumer charging cords. We built a better way. By designing every SlateSafe enclosure around our custom-engineered Flat USB-C Extension, we eliminated the internal bulk, allowing for a flush, low-profile fit that makes the tablet look like a native part of the wall or counter.

Technical Specifications

Profile

Power Delivery

Data Transfer
